FIELD MANUAL

Chess, with a third axis
and consequences.

01

Choose a piece

You play White. Select a piece, then choose a highlighted destination. Black responds automatically.

02

Rotate the rules

XY is familiar chess. XZ and YZ rotate movement into vertical planes. Pawns use them to climb or excavate walls.

03

Read the landing

Teal is safe, amber creates an impact or excavates, and red means the mover will perish.

04

Use gravity

Long falls shatter terrain or squash pieces. Towers travel together, shelves can collapse, and capturing or crushing a King wins.
